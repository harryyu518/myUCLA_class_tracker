Voter Registration

UCLA Bruins are involved in the community at all levels, including voting in local, state and national elections. Make your voice heard, register to vote.


You can register or re-register to vote. Re-registration is required if you have moved, changed name, or wish to change party affiliation. Out of State students preferring to register to vote in their home state can register or re-register to vote in their home state. For further information on registration and voting, visit UCLA Voter Resources and Information website .
